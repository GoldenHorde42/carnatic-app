Subject: Re: Carnatic App — ToS Violations Report V.1 — Compliance Response

Hi YouTube API Services Team,

Following up on my earlier note — all violations from the report have been
resolved as of March 12, 2026. Here is a full account of what was fixed:


── III.D.1c — Multiple project numbers ──────────────────────────────────────

We use a single Google Cloud project: #298276742704, covering both the iOS
and Android API clients. No additional project numbers are in use.


── III.A.1 — Terms of Use ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

A dedicated Terms of Use page is now live at:
  https://carnaticapp.org/terms.html

It is also accessible in-app via the Library tab. Both include the statement:

  "By using this App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service
  (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and Google's Privacy Policy."


── III.A.2e — Data sharing disclosure ───────────────────────────────────────

A "How We Share Your Information" section has been added to our Privacy
Policy (https://carnaticapp.org/privacy.html), identifying each party:
YouTube/Google (playback), Supabase (database infrastructure), and Groq
(anonymised search), along with the legal basis for each.


── III.A.2g — Device storage disclosure ─────────────────────────────────────

A "Device Storage and Local Data" section has been added to the Privacy
Policy, disclosing that the app stores an authentication session token and
user preferences in device-local storage, explaining its purpose, and how
to clear it (sign out or uninstall).


── III.E.4a-g — YouTube statistics refresh ──────────────────────────────────

The Privacy Policy now includes the following explicit statement:

  "YouTube statistics (view counts, etc.) cached in our database are
  refreshed at least every 30 days. Statistics that cannot be refreshed
  within 30 days are removed from our cache."

Our backend pipeline refreshes all video metadata from the YouTube API on
each scheduled run, ensuring ongoing compliance.


── III.F.2a,b — YouTube branding guidelines ─────────────────────────────────

The following changes are live in iOS build 10 / Android (released
March 12, 2026):

  • Removed custom red play-button badges (▶) from the Home screen header
    and the Library/Profile sign-in screen. These icons resembled the
    YouTube logo and have been fully removed.
  • Removed a similar badge from the website Privacy Policy header.
  • The "Powered by YouTube" footer on both the Home and Library screens
    now uses the standard YouTube icon and links to https://www.youtube.com.


── III.I.4 — Video title redirect to YouTube ────────────────────────────────

In the video player screen:

  • The video title is now tappable and opens the video directly on YouTube
    (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v={videoId}).
  • A dedicated "Watch on YouTube" button has been added to the player
    actions row, also linking to the YouTube URL.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All changes are live at https://carnaticapp.org and in the latest app
builds. Please don't hesitate to reach out if any screenshots or additional
information would be helpful.

Thank you again for your patience.
